# Runtime-card sources

The public runtime cards are compact summaries rather than copies of source
articles or dialogue. The main references used to establish public facts were:

- [Genshin Impact Wiki on Fandom](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Genshin_Impact_Wiki)
- [Fandom licensing policy](https://www.fandom.com/licensing)
- [原神WIKI on BWiki](https://wiki.biligame.com/ys/)
- Publicly released in-game story and character material from Genshin Impact

Fandom community text is generally available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license. BWiki was used primarily to compare
Chinese naming and public facts. Site-specific terms remain applicable.

The cards deliberately omit source excerpts, local research paths, audit
hashes, and training data. Corrections to factual summaries are welcome.
