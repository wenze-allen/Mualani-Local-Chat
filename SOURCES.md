# Knowledge-card sources

[简体中文](SOURCES.zh-CN.md)

The objective and Mualani-viewpoint cards record their own source objects,
including provider, page title, URL, language, revision ID, and source-text
hash. The principal public references are:

- [Genshin Impact Wiki on Fandom](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Genshin_Impact_Wiki)
- [Fandom licensing policy](https://www.fandom.com/licensing)
- [Genshin Impact Wiki on BWiki](https://wiki.biligame.com/ys/)
- publicly released in-game story and character material from Genshin Impact
- the dialogue structure distributed through the YuanShenResources project

Fandom was the primary reference for topic boundaries and English terminology.
BWiki was used for cross-language verification and pinned character-voice
records. Local dialogue data was used to determine what Mualani personally
witnessed, heard, or said. Objective public facts do not automatically become
Mualani's knowledge.

The repository does not mirror complete wiki pages or the source dialogue
archive. Complete impression cards may contain short evidence summaries or
excerpts together with their source type, document ID, dialogue IDs, and
revision reference. Site-specific and original-rightsholder terms remain
applicable.
